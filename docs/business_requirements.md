# Business Requirements

## Business Scenario

A multi-region e-commerce marketplace generates large volumes of customer,
order, product, seller, payment, review, and delivery data across its operations.

Leadership currently lacks a centralized analytics solution for monitoring
commercial and operational performance.

The objective of this project is to design an enterprise analytics platform
that integrates these datasets, standardizes business KPIs, identifies
operational issues, and provides decision-makers with an interactive
operations control tower.

## Key Stakeholders

- Executive Leadership
- Sales and Commercial Teams
- Operations Management
- Customer Experience Teams
- Logistics and Fulfillment Teams
- Seller Management Teams
- Data and Analytics Teams

## Business Questions

1. How are revenue and order volume changing over time?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which geographic regions contribute the most orders and revenue?
4. What percentage of orders are delivered late?
5. Which sellers have the highest levels of delayed deliveries?
6. How does delivery performance affect customer review scores?
7. What are the most commonly used payment methods?
8. Which customers demonstrate repeat purchasing behavior?
9. What operational factors are associated with poor customer satisfaction?
10. Which business areas require management attention?

## Project Objectives

- Integrate multiple operational datasets into a centralized analytics model.
- Assess and improve data quality before reporting.
- Develop standardized KPIs for sales, customers, sellers, and delivery operations.
- Identify trends, anomalies, and operational bottlenecks.
- Build an executive-level Power BI operations control tower.
- Automate data transformation and validation workflows.
- Design a scalable architecture that can later be extended into a cloud analytics platform.

## Success Criteria

The solution should allow business users to:

- Monitor major commercial and operational KPIs from a centralized dashboard.
- Analyze performance across time, geography, products, sellers, and customers.
- Identify late deliveries and operational bottlenecks.
- Investigate relationships between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
- Access consistent KPI definitions based on validated data.
- Reduce dependence on manual analysis of disconnected datasets.

## Project Context

This is an independent portfolio project simulating an enterprise consulting
analytics engagement using publicly available e-commerce data. It is not an
actual client engagement or an Accenture project.
